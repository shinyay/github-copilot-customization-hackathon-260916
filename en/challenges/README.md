## How to proceed

1. Read **Scenario** and **What this feature is** to understand the challenge and the feature's limits.
2. Copy only the materials specified by the README from `starter/` into your working repository.
3. Follow the steps in **Try it**.
4. If needed, use **Optional: Compare** to examine differences before and after customization.
5. Reflect on the result using **Verification points** and **Constraints, fallback, and safety**.

When a scenario has an `optional/` directory, you can explore additional product surfaces or operational conditions after completing the main scenario.
