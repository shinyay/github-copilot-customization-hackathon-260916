# Observe Organization Instructions within a limited scope

**Language:** [日本語](../../../../challenges/hc-039/optional/org-instructions-live.md) / **English**

[Return to the HC-039 main scenario](../README.md)

## Purpose

In an approved organization, observe the storage of organization Instructions, their delivery to a target surface, and their relationship with repository Instructions within a limited scope. This procedure does not prove that the text is always followed verbatim or enforced as organization policy.

## Prerequisites

- You can identify the target organization, repository, and surface.
- You can verify the current organization/repository Instructions text and revisions.
- You have organization owner permissions and eligibility for the target surface.
- The impact on other users, observation scope, stopping, and person responsible for restoration have been decided.

## Permissions and safety

- Obtain separate permission for storage/changes, limited observation, and effects on other users.
- Do not infer the real owner or expand the scope to ruleset or profile operations.
- Keep a restorable snapshot before overwriting existing text.
- Do not operate if you cannot identify only your own changes.

## Procedure

1. Record the target surface, current text, repository Instructions, revision, owner, and restoration method.
2. Review a minimal harmless instruction change and verify duplication and precedence relationships.
3. After approval, make the change once and observe the instructions delivered on a supported surface and the response.
4. Record storage success, instruction delivery, and reflection in the response separately.
5. Check for effects on other users, then restore the original state using the approved procedure.

## What to observe

- The text/revision of organization and repository Instructions
- Supported surface
- Result of the storage operation
- Scope of delivered instructions
- What was and was not confirmed in the response
- Impact on other users and restoration result

## Stop conditions

- The owner, target surface, current text, revision, or restoration scope is unknown.
- There is no approval for operations that affect other users.
- Duplication or precedence relationships with repository Instructions cannot be verified.
- You cannot safely restore only your own changes.
